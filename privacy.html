Sijil Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Sijil is a point-of-sale and bookkeeping app for shops. It is published by Khattab ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version: your shop's records stay on your phone. We do not collect your sales, products or customers, and the app contains no advertising and no analytics of our own. The one thing you send us is your shop's verification before subscribing: the owner's ID and the shop's licence, encrypted, as set out in section 3.

1. Information stored on your phone

Everything you enter into Sijil is stored only on the phone you enter it on: products and prices, stock, sales and receipts, returns, customers and what they owe, suppliers and deliveries, expenses and cash-drawer records, your shop's details and settings, and the documents you keep in the app (photos or files of the shop's papers and the owner's ID). Documents are kept inside the app, out of the phone's photo gallery, and are not sent to your other tills.

This information may include personal information about other people, such as your customers' and suppliers' names and phone numbers. You decide what to enter, and you are responsible for having the right to keep it (see section 7).

We cannot see, read or recover any of it, except what you send us to verify your shop (section 3). If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted from the phone, unless you have made a backup.

2. When information leaves your phone

Information leaves your phone only when you choose one of the following:

  • Backups. When you make a backup, the app writes a file containing your shop's records and lets you save or share it wherever you choose. The file is not encrypted. Keep it somewhere safe; anyone who has the file can read it. If you keep documents in the app, the file includes their photos unless you turn that off on the backup screen.
  • Multi-till sync (multi-till plan only). If you connect your tills to a sync server, records are sent to that server. The server is software that you or your shop runs on its own computer or host; it is not run by us, and we have no access to it.
  • WhatsApp and sharing. When you send a balance reminder or share a statement, the app opens WhatsApp or your phone's share menu with the message filled in. What happens next is governed by that app's own policy.
  • Printing. Receipts and reports are sent to the Bluetooth printer you connect.
  • Selling by voice. When you tap the microphone, the app asks your phone's speech-recognition service (usually provided by Google) to turn your speech into text. Sijil does not record or keep the audio. The speech service processes it under its provider's own policy.

3. Subscriptions and activation codes

  • Google Play. If you subscribe through Google Play, Google handles the payment. We do not receive your card or bank details. The app only receives whether a subscription is active and which plan it is, through Google Play.
  • Activation codes. The app shows a shop code. It is made from your phone's Android ID by a one-way calculation and cannot be turned back into that ID or into anything about you. If you buy an activation code from us or a reseller, you send this shop code. We keep a record of each code issued: the shop code, the plan, the number of days, the date, and any note about the payment (for example "paid by Bankak"), so that we can resend or check codes. We keep these records for as long as your codes may be in use and as required for our own accounting.
  • Shop verification. Before subscribing, you send the owner's ID card or passport, the shop's licence or commercial register (or a photo of the shop front), the owner's full name, and the shop's name, phone number, shop code and country, through an account you create with an email address. These are encrypted on your phone before they are sent, and stored in that encrypted form with our cloud provider, Supabase. Only we can open them, on our own computer, and we use them only to check that the shop and its owner are real and to prevent fraud. We do not share them with anyone, except where the law requires us to. We keep them while your subscription is active and for up to 12 months after it ends, then delete them. You can delete them sooner from the app ("Delete my cloud data") or by asking us; the shop may then need to be verified again before its next subscription.

4. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to scan barcodes. Images are processed on the phone and not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Bluetooth (nearby devices): to connect to a receipt printer already paired with the phone. The app does not ask for or use your location.
  • Internet: for multi-till sync with your shop's server and for Google Play subscriptions.

5. Information collected by others

  • The barcode scanner uses Google's ML Kit, which runs on the phone. Google may collect limited technical information about its use, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Play, WhatsApp, your speech-recognition service and your phone's operating system each have their own privacy policies.

We do not sell personal information and do not share it with advertisers.

6. Security

Your records are stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Automatic Android cloud backup is turned off so that your records do not leave the phone without your choice. Protect your phone with a screen lock, and keep backup files private.

7. Your customers' information

When you record customers or suppliers, you are responsible for that information. Collect only what you need, keep it accurate, and follow the data protection laws that apply to your shop. You can correct a customer's details, or archive them so they no longer appear in your lists, in the app.

8. Children

Sijil is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Your choices

  • Sales, payments and stock movements are kept as a permanent ledger so that your books can always be explained; mistakes are corrected with a new entry rather than deleted. You can remove all data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.
  • You can choose not to use sync, voice, WhatsApp or backups.
  • To ask about the activation-code records we keep about your shop code, or to have them deleted once you no longer use your codes, contact us.
  • You can delete your verification documents at any time, as set out in section 3, and delete documents kept in the app from the Documents screen.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
